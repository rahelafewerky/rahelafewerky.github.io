Journal Publications

Afewerky, R. K. (2023). Residual Responsibility to Implement: the AU, the Constitutive Act, and the Responsibility to Protect. Global Responsibility to Protect, 1(aop), 1-23.
[Publisher Link]

View Abstract

This article analyzes the African Union's (AU) challenges in implementing the Responsibility to Protect (R2P) amidst ongoing mass atrocities in Africa, exploring the gap between the AU's legal framework and its actual intervention capabilities.

Book Chapters

Afewerky, R. K. (2022). Darfur: R2P and dilemmas of implementation. In The Responsibility to Protect Twenty Years On: Rhetoric and Implementation (pp. 33-53). Cham: Springer International Publishing.
[Publisher Link]

View Abstract

Focusing on the Darfur case, this chapter examines the 20-year history of R2P implementation, analyzing the failure to prevent genocide, the impact of delayed responses, and the necessity of focusing on the "responsibility to rebuild" within the framework.
