--- layout: archive title: "Research" permalink: /publications/ author_profile: true --- ## Journal Publications **Afewerky, R. K.** (2023). "Residual Responsibility to Implement: the AU, the Constitutive Act, and the Responsibility to Protect." *Global Responsibility to Protect*, 1(aop), 1-23. [Publisher Link](https://brill.com/view/journals/gr2p/16/2/article-p127_002.xml)
Abstract Decades after the African Union adopted the Constitutive Act and the Responsibility to Protect (R2P), mass atrocities continue to go unchecked in Africa. This article analyses both the complementary features of the Constitutive Act and R2P, and the challenges impeding the AU from upholding its responsibilities.
--- ## Book Chapters **Afewerky, R. K.** (2022). "Darfur: R2P and dilemmas of implementation." In *The Responsibility to Protect Twenty Years On: Rhetoric and Implementation* (pp. 33-53). Cham: Springer International Publishing. [Publisher Link](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-90731-0_3)
Abstract This chapter focuses on the case of Darfur, analyzing the initiatives that curtailed a prompt intervention, the failure to eradicate existing tensions, and the consequences of the non-implementation of the responsibility to rebuild.
